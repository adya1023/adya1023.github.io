07 · Ideas explored · chosen vs dropped

DroppedSell Manjusha wall art

Why I dropped itAlready crowded, and it keeps the art on the wall instead of in daily life.

DroppedCopy motifs straight onto products

Why I dropped itLooks like souvenir merchandise and loses the art's rules.

DroppedShop by product category

Why I dropped itPeople shop for a need ("a gift", "for my desk"), not "trays".

ChosenEveryday objects + story-led store

Why I chose itMotif → abstraction → object keeps the meaning, and shopping by purpose matches how people buy.

08 · User flow

Homepage→Shop by purpose→Objects (filter by motif)→Product: story + lineage→Add to bag or buy now→Bag→Checkout→Order confirmed + story card

09 · Usability test plan

Tasks
  1. Find a gift under ₹1,500 for a friend who loves stories.
  2. Tell me the story behind the keepsake box.
  3. Buy it and get to order confirmed.
What I'll measure
  • Task success rate
  • Time on task
  • Can they retell the story? (comprehension)
  • SUS ease-of-use score
Who & how

5 gift buyers aged 22–35, 20-minute remote sessions with the clickable prototype, thinking aloud.

Goal: check that buyers understand the story and can buy without help.
